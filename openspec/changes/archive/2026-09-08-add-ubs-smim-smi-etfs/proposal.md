## Why

ETF Portfolio Lens already supports UBS constituent workbooks through the generic UBS XML-in-XLS parser, but the UBS SMIM and SMI ETFs are not registered and therefore cannot be ingested or represented in the catalog. The supplied constituent workbooks confirm that both funds use the existing UBS format and can be added without introducing a new parser.

## What Changes

- Register UBS SMIM ETF CHF dis (`CH0111762537`) with its fund-page URL, ticker metadata, and supplied fixture.
- Register UBS SMI ETF CHF acc (`CH1447931341`) with its fund-page URL, ticker metadata, and supplied fixture.
- Reuse `ubs_xml_xls_v1` and the existing `xls` download and normalization flow.
- Add deterministic fixture and snapshot tests for both registry entries.
- Validate that live product-page retrieval resolves a constituent workbook; preserve fixture mode if the UBS pages remain dynamic or cookie-gated.

## Capabilities

- `ubs-smim-smi-etfs`: Support registry metadata, constituent ingestion, and fixture validation for the UBS SMIM and SMI ETFs.
